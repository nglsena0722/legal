Quick help

  • Artworks gone? Your artworks are saved on your device. Reinstalling or clearing app data deletes them, so save the ones you want to keep to the Photos app.
  • Can’t save to Photos? Allow the app to add photos in your device settings, then try again.
  • Change language? The app follows your device language. Change it in your device settings.

Quick help

  • Best score gone? Your best score is saved on your device. Reinstalling or clearing app data resets it.
  • How do I shoot? Press and hold anywhere on the screen to charge, then let go. Hold longer to throw farther.
  • Change language? The game follows your device language. Change it in your device settings.
